Its role inside Home

The building's own intelligence layer.

Brain does not replace Home. It gives Home an on-site inference layer inside the building's governed architecture.

01 / CONTEXT

Grounded before inference

Brain receives the records selected by identity and retrieval. The model does not decide what a person is allowed to access.

02 / LOCAL

Inside the building

The local model runs on the building's own device. Cloud inference remains explicit and optional rather than required.

03 / BOUNDARY

Controlled egress

The Home architecture makes the allowed paths out of the building visible: payment token, visitor pass and encrypted backup.
