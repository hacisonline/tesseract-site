Tesseract Twin

Turn a project into a decision model.

Twin is easiest to understand as a pipeline: inputs go in, the linked model connects them, and the output is a decision-ready view of risk, return, and pressure points.

Inputs

Project assumptions

Land, zone, road width, FSI, TDR, approvals, costs, ASP, sales, debt, equity, and stage timing.

Model

Linked feasibility engine

Regulatory assumptions, capital stack, construction phasing, pre-sales, shortfall, and delay pressure move together.
