## Positioning

Keep **Real Estate Intelligence**. Focus entirely on the real-estate ecosystem. Do not include Tesseract Fleet.

## Product hierarchy

- **Tesseract Twin** is the development-strategy product and must reflect its latest verified product state.
- **Tesseract Home** is the building-intelligence product and the primary lived-building experience.
- **Tesseract Brain** is cutting-edge upcoming hardware within the Tesseract Home ecosystem. It runs Home's on-site intelligence. It must not be presented as a generic grey peer card or as a thin floating-logo page.
- **The Presidential** is Home's first live deployment and case study. Keep the mention small and subordinate to Home.

## Non-negotiable experience decisions

1. Preserve the canonical Tesseract opening scroll animation and exact logo geometry.
2. The existing live Home system architecture must become the **Tesseract Home page hero**, not a secondary CTA or detached feature.
3. The architecture is already mobile-optimized and is the clearest expression of Home's pipeline. Design around it rather than simplifying it into decorative orbit labels.
4. Brain needs genuine hardware-product marketing: physical presence, local intelligence, privacy, deployment context and a sense of an upcoming frontier. Do not invent specifications, performance claims or availability dates.
5. Avoid generic product grids, grey hierarchy, static logo hero treatments, decorative bloom spots and template-like AI gradients.
6. Buttons, lighting and backgrounds must be intentional and clean—no accidental-looking bloom artifacts.
7. The Presidential remains proof, not a product category.

## Brand canon

- Exact outer blue isometric cube plus inner green cube.
