The Privacy Boundary

The building keeps its own counsel.

Tesseract Home is built so that intelligence does not require surrender. Questions are answered inside the building, over the building's own records.

Identity gate binds every request to the person asking. Deny by default. Grounding is retrieval-first: below the evidence threshold, the system says so. Local inference is the default mode. Cloud is explicit, optional, and logged. An audit actor is recorded on every write. Reception exchanges are captured.
