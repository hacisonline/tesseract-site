Role-Aware by Design
Different surfaces. One building.

Each person sees the part of the building they are allowed to act on. Every surface reads and writes through the same twin.

◆ Residents

Visitors, amenities, bills, helpdesk, parking, valet and notices through the resident app.

◆ Building-Team Desks

Gate pass and OTP flows, entry and exit logs, valet retrieval and facilities operations.

◆ Management

A management view over the building's operating model, with actions attributed and auditable.

◆ Sensors

Building signals ingest into the same twin that supports the people and operating surfaces.

Privacy is an architecture,
not a promise.

Identity, grounding and local inference define what a request can see before an answer is formed.

Identity Gate

Every request is bound to the person and the units they are allowed to access, deny by default.

Grounding First

Answers are formed over retrieved building records, with an honest miss below the evidence threshold.

Tesseract Brain

The local model runs on the building's own device; cloud inference is optional rather than required.
